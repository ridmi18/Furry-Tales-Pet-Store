About Furry Tales

At Furry Tales, we help you find the perfect pet and provide excellent care. We offer a range of pets, including dogs, cats, birds, and small animals. Our services also include professional grooming to keep your pets looking and feeling their best. Our grooming options include basic, deluxe, premium, bath & brush, nail trim & ear cleaning, puppy’s first grooming, older pet care, and custom grooming. Furry Tales is where your journey to a new furry friend and their care begins.
